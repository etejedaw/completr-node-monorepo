Ratio & Personal Ratio

Completr's signature metric. How much score a game delivers per hour. Higher = better value.

Ratio = (scorenorm / duration) × 20

scorenorm = public score normalized to 0–5 (so 82/100 → 4.1). Practical max ratio: ~100.

tips_and_updates Personal Ratio

Same idea, but personalized to you. The regular ratio uses the public score and the average duration. The personal ratio uses your own rating (how much you actually liked the game) and how long it really took you to finish it.

It only appears once you complete a run — and tells you the honest, after-the-fact value the game had for you.

Your collection modules

Beyond the backlog, five more modules to organize your collection:

shelves My Shelf (Game Shelf)

Your physical and digital collection — what you actually own. Track editions, acquired date and platform.

playlist_play Up Next (Queue)

Runs you want to play soon, from your own backlog. Auto-removes on complete/abandon.

favorite_border Want to Get (Wishlist)

Games you want to obtain or buy — you don't own them yet. Optional platform tag.

star Favorites

Your all-time favorites. No backlog needed — you can favorite anything.

format_list_bulleted Lists

Curated collections of games you build and (optionally) share — sagas, top picks, tournaments. Other users can follow your lists.
