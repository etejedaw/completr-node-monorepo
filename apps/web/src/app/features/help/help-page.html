Guide

Runs, the ratio, scores and privacy, explained in one page. Read it top to bottom or jump to what you need.

01 · My Games

Every game you track is a run

A run is one playthrough: its status, platform, dates, your rating, the hours it really took and your notes or review. One game can have several runs, like PS5 now and a replay on Switch years later.

02 · Ratio

How much a game gives back per hour

The ratio is Completr's signature number. It weighs how good a game is against how long it takes to beat. Higher means more value per hour, which makes it a great way to choose what to play next.

Score ÷ divided by Hours × times 20 = equals Ratio

The score is first brought to a 0–5 scale, so 82/100 becomes 4.1. A five-star game you beat in one hour sits at 100, the practical maximum.

Your personal ratio

When you complete a run, Completr runs the same math with your rating and your real hours. The arrow tells you if the game was worth more or less to you than estimated.

03 · Scores

Where a game's score comes from

Every game page shows one score card. Completr uses the most reliable source it has, in this order:

The Completr score needs ratings and real hours from enough players who finished the game. Until then, the card shows Metacritic with HowLongToBeat, or RAWG when those are missing.

06 · Saved Views

Filters you use every day, one tap away

Set up My Games the way you like (status, platform, dates, rating, sort) and save it with a name. Two options make a view even quicker to reach:

star Default
Opens automatically every time you visit My Games.
push_pin Pinned
Shows up as a tab at the top of My Games, so you can switch with one click.
bookmark Open Saved Views
07 · Privacy

You decide who sees what

Choose a level for your whole profile, or fine-tune each section on its own: My Games, My Shelf, Lists, Up Next, Want to Get, Favorites and your activity in other people's feeds.

  • public Public

    Anyone, even visitors without an account.

  • group Friends

    Only people you follow and who follow you back.

  • lock Private

    Only you. Hidden sections don't appear on your profile at all.
