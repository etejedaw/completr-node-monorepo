Ratio & Personal Ratio

Completr's signature metric. The Ratio tells you how much score a game delivers per hour, so you can prioritize what to play next. Higher = more value per hour.

Ratio = (scorenorm / duration) × 20

scorenorm is the public score normalized to a 0–5 scale (so 82/100 → 4.1, 8.2/10 → 4.1, etc.). With this normalization, the practical max ratio is around 100.

Score 82 · 100h · (4.1/5 × 20 ÷ 100)

Same score, but spread over 100h. Much lower ratio.

tips_and_updates Personal Ratio

Same formula, but using your rating and your real duration (not the estimate). The honest, after-the-fact version. Only appears once you complete a run.

Aggregate Score vs Completr Score

Two different scores you'll see across the app.

Aggregate Score 82

The canonical public score for a game. Picked from external sources (Metacritic, OpenCritic, RAWG) — Completr chooses the most reputable one available.

Completr Score Soon

Future. Will aggregate the ratings of Completr users who actually completed the game, weighted by reputation.

Statuses

Every backlog run has one of four statuses. These are the actual badges you'll see in the app:

radio_button_unchecked Not started You own it, haven't played yet.
play_arrow Playing Currently in progress.
check_circle Completed Finished. Unlocks Personal Ratio and reviews.
cancel Abandoned Dropped, won't finish it.

Queue, Wishlist, Favorites, Lists

Four modules, four different purposes. Their sidebar icons are shown below:

playlist_play Queue

Runs you want to play soon, picked from your own Backlog. Priority ranking of games you already own. Auto-removed when a run is completed or abandoned.

favorite_border Wishlist

Games you want to obtain or buy — you don't have them yet. Optional platform tag for the version you want.

star Favorites

Your all-time favorite games. No backlog required — you can favorite something you've never played.

format_list_bulleted Lists

Curated collections you build and (optionally) share. Sagas, top picks, themed sets — other users can follow your lists.
