02 · Ratio

How much a game gives back per hour

The ratio is Completr's signature number. It weighs how good a game is against how long it takes to beat. Higher means more value per hour, which makes it a great way to choose what to play next.

Score (1–5) ÷ divided by Hours × times 20 = equals Ratio

Score, on a 1–5 scale
Every source rates on its own scale, so scores are normalized first to fit 1–5: 82/100 becomes 4.1 and 6/7 becomes 4.29. A score that is already out of 5 is used as is.
Why × 20
Once the score is on 1–5, the result is multiplied by 20 so it reads as a 0–100 number. Bigger numbers leave more room to tell games apart: a five-star game you beat in one hour sits at 100, the practical maximum.

Your personal ratio

When you complete a run, Completr runs the same math with your rating and your real hours. The arrow tells you if the game was worth more or less to you than estimated.
