08 · Privacy

You decide who sees what

Choose a level for your whole profile, or fine-tune each section on its own: My Games, My Shelf, Platinums, Sagas, Rewind, Up Next, Want to Get, Favorites, Lists and your activity in other people's feeds.

Your profile is the front door: people who can't open it see none of its sections. Your Rewind is the one exception: anyone with its link can open it at its own level, even if your profile is private, but it never appears in your profile or in your archive of years, and your platinums stay hidden.

  • public Everyone

    Anyone, even visitors without an account.

  • group Friends

    Only people you follow and who follow you back.

  • lock Only you

    Hidden from everyone else. Hidden sections don't appear on your profile at all.
