07 · Rewind

Your year in games, once a year

Rewind is a summary of one calendar year of your runs: the games you finished, dropped and started, the hours you played, your highlights, a persona that sums up how you played, a month-by-month timeline and a few charts. You get one Rewind per year.

auto_awesome You generate it
Nothing happens on its own. From your profile, press Generate your Rewind. Until then nobody else can see that year.
event Open for a season
Each year's Rewind opens for a season. Generating, regenerating and adding an AI recap only work while it's open, and meanwhile that year's Rewind is featured on your profile. A year you didn't generate during its season can't be generated later. With Premium you can also generate earlier years, from the year you joined and with games started or finished in them.
translate Language
Pick English or Spanish when you generate it. Everyone who opens your Rewind reads it in that language.
workspace_premium AI recap
An AI-written summary and extra charts, with Premium or a Rewind gift. When generating, two switches decide whether it includes your private runs and uses your notes. Without either, your Rewind is generated once and its options stay as they are.
autorenew AI generations
Every use of the AI spends one AI generation of that year's Rewind, the first one too: generating with the recap, regenerating to change the options or adding the recap later. How many you get depends on your access. During a short grace window inside the season you get one extra for the current year, and if the AI fails the generation is given back.
ac_unit Frozen when it closes
When the season ends, every Rewind generated during it is kept as it was at that moment: games you add later or a run that changes privacy don't change it anymore. Hiding your Rewind or your Platinums still applies right away.
ios_share Sharing
Every Rewind has an image made for stories and a link preview. Use Share, Download or Copy link from the Rewind page.
