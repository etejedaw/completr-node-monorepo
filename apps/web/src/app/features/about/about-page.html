About

A game backlog tracker in the spirit of Trakt and Letterboxd, built around one question: what should you play next?

menu_book Read the guide

Too many games. Not enough time.

Completr weighs every game's score against the hours it takes to beat. That number, the ratio, puts the games that give you the most per hour at the top of your backlog.

Track every run, follow friends, build lists and see what's worth your next evening.
