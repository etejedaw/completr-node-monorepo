## Who runs Completr

Completr is a personal project built and operated by **Esteban Tejeda**, who acts as the data controller for the data described here. This policy covers both the sign-up page (completr.app) and the application (web.completr.app).

The idea is simple: I collect the minimum needed for the app to work, I never sell your data or share it for commercial purposes, and you can ask for a copy or for everything to be deleted whenever you want.

The people whose data I process are those who request an invitation and those who create an account in the app. You provide all of it yourself: I don't buy databases or get your information from third parties.

## What data I collect

**Invitation request.** If you request an invitation from the home page, I only store your email address, to let you know when a spot opens up.

**Your account.** When you sign up I store your email, an encrypted version of your password (never plain text), your username and, optionally, a display name, bio and avatar.

**Content you create.** What you track inside the app: your game backlog, lists, reviews, tags, progress notes, co-op runs, platinums and the yearly Rewinds you generate. It's the whole point of the service.

**Technical data.** Aggregate usage statistics that don't identify you (see below), and server logs I keep to detect failures and attacks.

## What I use it for

- Run the service and show you your own content.
- Authenticate you and keep your session secure.
- Understand in aggregate how the app is used, to improve it.
- Generate your Rewind when you ask for it, including its AI-written summary when your account has it.
- Contact you about your account or your invitation.

What I **don't** do: I don't sell or share your data for commercial purposes, I don't run ads, I don't sign you up to newsletters and I don't build profiles about you, apart from the Rewind you generate yourself (see "Automated decisions" below).

## Legal basis

I process your account data and your content to provide the service you request when you sign up (performance of a contract, article 13(c) of Chile's Law 21.719). Aggregate analytics and security logs rely on my legitimate interest in keeping the app running and improving it (article 13(d)). The Rewind, including its AI summary, is part of the service you request when you generate it (article 13(c)). Your notes and your private runs only go into it if you turn on the matching switch for that Rewind. The invitation request relies on your consent (article 12).

You can withdraw that consent whenever you want, without giving a reason and at no cost: email me and I'll remove your address from the list. Withdrawing it doesn't affect what I did before with your permission.

## Cookies and analytics

To understand how the app is used I use **Umami**, an analytics tool I host myself that measures visits in an aggregate, anonymous way. It uses no cookies, doesn't track across sites and doesn't collect personally identifiable information.

The app uses a single cookie: the one strictly necessary so you don't have to sign in on every page. It can't be used to track you and it can't be disabled without keeping you out of your account, which is why you won't see cookie consent banners.

## What other users see

Completr is a social app: part of what you track is meant to be seen by others. You decide how much, but it's worth knowing how it works.

Each section of your account (profile, My Games, My Shelf, Platinums, Sagas, Rewind, Up Next, Want to Get, Favorites, Lists and your activity feed) has its own visibility level, and you can change each one separately whenever you want:

- **Everyone:** anyone can see it, even someone without a Completr account.
- **Friends:** only people you follow who also follow you back can see it.
- **Only you:** nobody else can see it.

**By default those eleven sections are visible to everyone.** It's a design decision: an app for sharing backlogs with nothing visible isn't much use. But it means that if you don't change anything, your profile and what you track are visible on the internet. If you'd rather have the opposite, you can set everything to Only you from the settings.

There are a few exceptions, and they go in opposite directions:

- **Lists start private.** Every list you create starts out as yours alone; you publish it separately when you decide to.
- **A Rewind stays hidden until you generate it.** Even with its section set to Everyone, nobody sees a year's Rewind until you create it from your profile. Whoever can see it also sees its AI summary, which may paraphrase your notes if you included them but never quotes them.
- **Reviews have no visibility control.** If you write a review or rate a game, anyone with a Completr account can see it on that game's page, even if the rest of your profile is private. If you don't want something to be seen, don't post it as a review.

## Where your data goes

I don't hand it to anyone for commercial purposes. But for the app to work, your data goes through this infrastructure, and I'd rather name it plainly:

- **Cloudflare** (United States). Everything you send and receive goes through its network before reaching my servers, so technically it can see it.
- **My servers** (São Paulo, Brazil). The application, the database and the analytics run here. I manage them directly.
- **Email service** (São Paulo, Brazil). It receives the email address you leave when you request an invitation.
- **DeepSeek** (China). It writes the AI summary of the Rewind. It only receives data when you generate a Rewind that includes that summary, and only the data for that year: your games, with their dates, platform, genres, hours and your ratings, and the statistics calculated from them. Your notes and your private runs only go if you turn on their switches. It receives nothing that identifies you: no email, username, display name or account ID, only your runs. The request is sent from my servers, so it doesn't see your IP address either. The only exception is anything identifying you write yourself in your notes, and only if you choose to include them. DeepSeek stores and processes that data in China. Its terms don't set how long it keeps what it receives through its API or say whether it uses it to train its models: its general policy allows that use for its own services and lets you object by writing to privacy@deepseek.com. Since the request carries nothing that identifies you, that copy isn't linked to you.

Video game data comes from external catalogs and sites (such as RAWG, IGDB, Metacritic, HowLongToBeat and Wikidata), but that's information I _receive_: I never send your personal data to those services.

Beyond those and the image servers described next, there are no third parties. Fonts and icons are served from my own servers, so your browser doesn't connect to Google or any external CDN for them. Game images (covers and backgrounds) are loaded directly from the RAWG and IGDB image servers: your browser connects to them to download the images and, as with any connection, they see your IP address and browser, but they don't receive any other data about you.

## Transfers outside Chile

My servers are in Brazil and Cloudflare is a US service, so your data leaves Chile. Those transfers are covered by the data processing agreements signed with each provider, which include contractual clauses with protection safeguards.

When you generate a Rewind with the AI summary, the data listed for DeepSeek also leaves Chile, to China. That transfer only happens when you ask for that summary, because the summary can't be written without it, and it's limited to what the summary needs.

## Automated decisions

No automated system makes decisions about your account: nothing about what you can do in Completr depends on one. I don't use your data to train models.

The only profile is the Rewind, and it exists only if you ask for it. When you generate it, the app assigns you a persona (such as Trophy Hunter or The Finisher) based on your numbers for the year and, with the AI summary, DeepSeek writes a description of your habits as a player. It has no effect on your account, you decide who sees it from its privacy section, and you can regenerate it to replace it.

## How long I keep it

- **Your account and your content:** for as long as you want. There's no maximum period and no automatic deletion for inactivity: your backlog stays there until you ask me to delete your account.
- **Copy of your data:** if you request one, the file stays available to download in Settings → Your data for 7 days after I approve the request, then it's deleted.
- **Record of your requests:** I keep a record of each copy or deletion request, with no personal data, only as proof that I answered it.
- **Invitation request email:** until you ask me to delete it, or at most 12 months after registration opens.
- **Your Rewinds:** stored with your account, AI summary included, until you delete the account. Regenerating a Rewind replaces the previous version.
- **What DeepSeek receives:** I don't control how long it keeps it. Its terms set no period and I can't ask it to delete your data in particular, so deleting your Completr account doesn't remove that copy.
- **Server logs:** 30 days, then they're deleted.
- **Analytics:** aggregate and anonymous, so it isn't tied to you and there's nothing to delete.

When I approve the deletion of your account, it's permanent: I delete the account and all its content (backlog, lists, reviews, followers, activity, Rewinds and the rest) and I don't keep backups, so no copy of you survives somewhere else. The only thing that remains is the record of the request, with no personal data. The other exception is what DeepSeek received when you generated an AI summary, described above.

## Your rights

You can exercise these rights over your personal data at no cost and without having to explain why. They're recognized by Chile's Law 21.719 and, if you're in the European Union, by the GDPR.

- **Access.** Know what data of yours I have and what I do with it.
- **Rectification.** Correct anything that's wrong or incomplete.
- **Erasure.** Ask me to delete it.
- **Objection.** Ask me to stop processing it.
- **Portability.** Receive it in a format you can take with you.

You can edit your data directly from the app. For everything else, use Settings → Your data or email me at [completr@etejeda.dev](mailto:completr@etejeda.dev):

- **A copy of your data** (access and portability). It's a structured JSON file with everything of yours, which you can take to another service. Once approved, you download it from the same section for 7 days.
- **Deleting your account** (erasure). It's permanent and can't be undone.
- **Rectification or objection.** Email me.

I review each request myself and reply within 30 calendar days. If I need more time, I can extend that period once, by another 30 days, letting you know beforehand. When I resolve it, I'll let you know by email.

If I don't reply in time or I reject your request, you can file a complaint with Chile's Personal Data Protection Agency.

## Security

Your password is stored encrypted: not even I can read it. Everything traveling between your device and Completr is encrypted, and your session is protected so other sites can't take advantage of it. Access to the servers and the database is restricted and managed only by me. No system is foolproof, but I treat your data with the same care I'd want for my own.

If a breach affecting your data ever happens, I'll tell you and report it to the Agency, as the law requires.

## Minors

Completr is not directed at children under 14, the age below which Chilean law considers a person a child and requires permission from their parents or guardians (in the European Union the threshold goes up to 16). If you believe a minor has provided me with data, email me and I'll delete it.
